Wavenum privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026. Wavenum is made by Kishan Tiwari (kishantiwari@iitkgp.ac.in).

Desktop app

The desktop app (including the Microsoft Store version) processes your spectra on your computer. It collects no personal data, has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or self-updater, works offline, and sends none of your files anywhere. Files are saved only where you choose; settings and your own libraries stay in your user profile.

It connects to the internet only when you ask it to: opening a web link in your browser, or downloading the optional open reference databases (RRUFF and others) from their public servers. Those servers see an ordinary download request.

Website

On wavenum.vercel.app, files you open are sent to the app's server only to be processed. The website is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard access logs, and uses Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics to count visits (pages viewed, browser, approximate location). No spectra or file contents are sent to Google. The desktop app does not load these.
